Registration forms allow you to bring in new advocates and collect key information about them to store in Quorum. The information that you can collect is customizable through choosing which fields and in what order to display on the form. To get started, click "+ Add Field to Form" below and select from basic fields that we have prepopulated and custom fields that your team has made. Drag and drop to reorder how they will display on the form and click the pencil to edit their settings.
